Berry was named the Walt Halperin Endowed Professor of Biology in Sept 2026. We are incredibly grateful to Mike and Jodi Halperin for funding this Professorship, and we are inspired by the example of Walt Halperin in both research and life. Walt was a beloved professor in the Department of Botany, and later the Department of Biology.
